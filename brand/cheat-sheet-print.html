Universal patterns

Drop in the tone framework

Paste your Tone Framework (Formality 1–5, Warmth 1–5, Authority 1–5, Directness 1–5) at the top of every project so output is calibrated to your voice.

Pair agents with plugins

Complaint Handler + Escalation Matrix · Cancellation Save + Churn Signals · Review Generator + Review Handling Playbook.

Customer context first

Always lead the prompt with WHO the customer is. Generic prompts produce generic output.

Iterate, don't restart

If output isn't right: "shorter", "less formal", "more direct", "drop the discount offer". Next iteration is usually right.
