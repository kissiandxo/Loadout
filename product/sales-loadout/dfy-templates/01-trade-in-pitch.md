# DFY Template #1 — Trade-In Pitch

**Use when:** You're emailing a customer their trade-in offer for the first time, OR responding to "what's my [current car] worth?"

**Channel:** Email
**Length:** ~150 words
**Calibrated for:** Customer who hasn't been told the number yet — make the number feel fair, not low

---

## The template

**Subject:** Trade-in number on your [Year Make Model]

**Body:**

Hi [Customer first name],

Got the trade-in figure back on your [Year Make Model Trim] — **$[X]**, valid for 14 days from inspection.

Quick breakdown so the number makes sense:

- Base wholesale benchmark for similar [Year Model]: $[BASE]
- + Full service history (lifts buyer confidence): +$[X]
- + One-owner from new: +$[X]
- − Higher-than-average kilometres: -$[X]
- − Tyres at ~[N]% — we'll need to replace: -$[X]
- − Minor scratch on [panel] — touch-up cost: -$[X]

Final offer: **$[FINAL]**.

A couple of things you can do to move the number up: if you'd like to replace the tyres yourself before bringing it in, the -$[X] adjustment comes back. Same with the touch-up — your call.

Happy to walk through it on the phone whenever suits.

— [Your first name]

---

## Why this works

- **Numbers, not adjectives.** The breakdown removes the "they're lowballing me" reaction because every adjustment is defensible.
- **Gives them control.** The "you can fix the tyres yourself" sentence converts a take-it-or-leave-it offer into a negotiation they have agency in.
- **14-day validity.** Trade values move with the market — anchoring 14 days protects you and feels fair to them.

## When to customise

- **They already have a Redbook / private-sale number in mind** — add one line: "For reference, this sits at the realistic trade-in end of the Redbook range. Private sale could net you ~$[X] more but typically takes [N] weeks."
- **They've explicitly said budget is tight** — lead with the bottom line: "Your trade brings the net cash-to-find on the new [vehicle] down to $[Z]" — pivot to deal math, not trade math.
- **The trade is older than 8 years / over 150k km** — soften the wholesale benchmark line: "The market on [Year Model] right now is around $[BASE], which is partly age and partly demand for that variant."

## What NOT to do

- Don't quote the final number BEFORE the breakdown. People who hear the figure first will dispute every adjustment afterward.
- Don't email this without seeing the car first — pre-inspection numbers that turn out wrong destroy trust faster than any other communication.
- Don't apologise for the figure. It is what it is — defend it with the structure.
