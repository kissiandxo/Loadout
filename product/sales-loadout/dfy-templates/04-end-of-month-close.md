# DFY Template #4 — End-of-Month Close

**Use when:** A deal has been verbal-yes-ish for a week or more and you're in the last 3–5 days of the month, needing to close.

**Channel:** SMS or phone (NOT email — too easy to ignore at this stage)
**Length:** SMS ~2 sentences, phone opener ~15 seconds
**Calibrated for:** Real time-pressure that's true, not manufactured

---

## The truth filter — read this first

End-of-month closes work ONLY when the urgency is real. "We need to hit our number" is YOUR pressure, not theirs. Manufacturing urgency they don't share burns trust faster than any other dealership behaviour.

Real EOM urgencies you can lean on:
- **Manufacturer-backed promotion ends [date]** — drive-away rebate, free first 5 services, 0% finance offer. If your dealer is rolling one of these, this is real.
- **EOFY / EOM stock allocation** — if a specific colour/trim is sold-to-spec next month, the gap might be 6–8 weeks. Real for buyers with a delivery deadline.
- **Their own life deadline** — they mentioned needing it by [event] (road trip, kids' school start, lease handback). YOU're not pushing — you're respecting THEIR timeline.

If none of these are true, don't run this template. Run a regular `/follow-up` instead.

---

## Variant A — SMS (for warm but undecided)

Hi [Customer first name], quick one — the [manufacturer promotion] on the [vehicle] you're considering ends [day]. If we lock it in [today/tomorrow], you keep the [$N savings / inclusion]. If you'd rather wait, totally fine — just didn't want you to miss it without knowing. Either way let me know.

— [Your first name]

---

## Variant B — SMS (for verbal-yes, paperwork stalling)

Hi [Customer first name], [Your first name] from the dealership. Got the [vehicle] all prepped for collection — only thing missing is your green light on the finance docs. Quick reply when you can, otherwise I'll give you a buzz in the morning.

— [Your first name]

---

## Variant C — Phone opener (their own deadline)

> "Hi [Customer first name], [Your first name] from the dealership. Quick one — got the [vehicle] in the prep queue for [their deadline]. To get it ready in time, paperwork really needs to be signed by [3 days before]. You comfortable to come in [day/time] to sign, or shall we book a different slot?"

---

## Variant D — Phone opener (manufacturer promotion)

> "Hi [Customer first name], [Your first name] from the dealership. The [promotion name] on the [vehicle] ends [day] — I didn't want you to miss it if you're still planning to go ahead. Want to lock it in today, or shall I just keep you posted on the next round of offers when they come through?"

---

## Why these work

- **They give the customer an out.** "If you'd rather wait, totally fine" / "shall I just keep you posted" — the out is what makes the urgency feel real instead of manipulative. Counterintuitively, this lifts conversion vs hard-pressure framings.
- **Variant C and D use specific dates / events.** Vague "soon" or "this month" reads as a sales tactic. Specific "Friday at 5pm" reads as factual.
- **No fake scarcity.** None of these claim "only one left" or "I have another buyer" unless that's specifically and provably true. Inventing scarcity on a $50k+ purchase is the fastest way to lose the deal AND get a Google review you'll regret.

## What NOT to do

- **Don't fabricate a deadline.** Customers smell it. If there's no real EOM lever, this template doesn't apply — use a regular follow-up instead.
- **Don't run this on first-time buyers in the same way as repeat customers.** First-timers need EXTRA reassurance, not extra pressure. Soften every line by 20% if it's their first major purchase.
- **Don't combine with a "$X off if you sign today" hail-mary discount.** Either the urgency is the manufacturer offer (real) or it's a discount you invented (transparent). Pick one structure, not both.
- **Don't email this.** EOM closes need real-time channels — SMS or phone only. Email lands 6 hours later when the moment has passed.

## When to escalate

If the verbal-yes deal is genuinely about to collapse on EOM silence, escalate to a BM call rather than a third SMS from yourself. A "the BM and I both want to make sure you're not stuck on anything" call carries weight that another rep-level chase doesn't.
