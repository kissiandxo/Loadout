`@skills/10-customer-recap/SKILL.md`

Apply it to this interaction:

$ARGUMENTS

The rep will typically dump a rambling description of what happened. Parse it. If it's really thin, ask one question only: "What did they actually say they were stuck on, and what did you agree to do next?" Don't grill them.

Produce: the structured recap per the agent's output format — where they are in the deal, headline, what they said (their actual quotes where possible), what they want, what was agreed (they will / you will), what's still open, your read, and the next-action block with channel + timing + drafted message if applicable. End with a Notes block calling out internal chases needed (BM / parts / finance).

Format priorities: facts over vibes, attributed quotes for multi-person conversations, specific next-action over vague "follow up."
