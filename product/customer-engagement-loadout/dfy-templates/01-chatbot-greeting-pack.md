# DFY Template #1 — Chatbot Greeting Pack

**Use when:** Setting up the opening greeting for your Website Chatbot. The first message a visitor sees defines whether they engage or close the chat.

**Channel:** Chatbot widget
**Length:** 1-2 sentences each
**Calibrated for:** 6 different business types — pick the one closest to yours

---

## Variant A — E-commerce / DTC

**Use case:** Online store, physical products, support + lead capture.

> Hi — [Brand] here. Browsing or after something specific? Happy to help with sizing, shipping, returns, or anything else. While you're here, want me to send you a welcome code for first orders? Just say yes or skip.

**Why it works:** Names the brand, offers two paths (browsing or specific), surfaces the lead capture without forcing it.

---

## Variant B — SaaS / product

**Use case:** Software, signed-in users + prospects.

> Hi — [Product] support here. If you're a customer, tell me your account and I'll look it up. If you're checking us out, ask anything you'd like — pricing, features, comparison with [common competitor]. Either way, real questions land with a real answer.

**Why it works:** Distinguishes customer vs prospect (different intents), names the common competitor proactively, signals quality ("real answer").

---

## Variant C — Professional services / consulting

**Use case:** Higher-ticket service businesses, B2B.

> Hi — thanks for visiting [Firm]. I can help with questions about our services, share examples of similar work, or set up a 15-minute call with [Founder name] if a conversation makes more sense. What would be most useful?

**Why it works:** Signals expertise + accessibility, offers a real next step (15-min call with named individual), respects the visitor's time.

---

## Variant D — Hospitality / local services

**Use case:** Restaurants, salons, clinics, gyms, local services.

> G'day — [Business] chat. Booking, hours, menu/services, parking, gift vouchers — any of that, just ask. If you'd rather book straight in, link below: [booking link].

**Why it works:** Direct, lists the actual common questions (no guessing), shortcuts the most common intent (booking) immediately.

---

## Variant E — Educational / course / community

**Use case:** Online courses, communities, membership sites.

> Hi — [Course/Community] welcome bot. Curious about what's inside, who it's for, or the timeline? Or already a member with a login issue? Tell me which and I'll either answer or get you to the right human.

**Why it works:** Surfaces the three most common queries explicitly, distinguishes pre-purchase vs member, no marketing fluff.

---

## Variant F — Tech / API / developer-focused

**Use case:** Developer tools, APIs, technical products.

> Hi — [Product] support. Docs question, API issue, pricing, or wanting to talk to engineering? I can handle the first three; engineering convos I route to [team]. Drop in the details.

**Why it works:** Acknowledges what AI can / can't handle, names the routing, respects developer time (no fluff).

---

## When to customise

- **Multi-language audience:** Detect browser language, swap greeting accordingly. Don't try to handle multilingual in a single greeting — looks crowded.
- **Returning visitor (you can detect via cookie):** Drop the brand name reintroduction. "Hi again — what brings you back?"
- **Specific landing page context** (visitor arrived from an ad for product X): Reference it. "Hi — saw you came in via the [Product X] page. Questions about it specifically?"
- **High-stakes B2B:** Skip the lead capture in the greeting. Hold it for after you've answered their first question.

## What NOT to do

- Don't open with "Welcome to [Brand]! How can we help you today?" — universally cringed-at.
- Don't use exclamation marks (more than one in two sentences reads as too eager).
- Don't include emoji unless your brand is genuinely playful.
- Don't ask "what's your name and email" as the first thing — friction before value.
- Don't reference a "team" if it's actually just one person. Be honest.
- Don't promise "instant response" if the chatbot's fallback is a 24-hour email reply. Match expectations.
